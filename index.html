Getting around

  • London ⇄ Liverpool: direct Avanti West Coast trains from London Euston to Liverpool Lime Street, a bit over two hours.
  • London ⇄ Manchester: direct Avanti West Coast trains from London Euston to Manchester Piccadilly, roughly two and a half hours on the slower services.
  • Liverpool ⇄ Manchester: direct trains between Lime Street and Manchester Piccadilly take around 50 to 57 minutes.
  • Match days: trains and trams get packed around kickoff. Book tickets ahead, aim to arrive in town a couple of hours early, and check times on the day (rail engineering and disruption do happen).
  • Inside the cities: London has the Tube, with Fulham Broadway (Stamford Bridge) and Putney Bridge (Craven Cottage) both on the District line. Manchester uses the Metrolink tram, with Old Trafford on the Altrincham line.

About Diwali

🪔 Sunday, November 8, 2026

Diwali is the festival of lights, celebrated by Hindus, Sikhs and Jains. It marks new beginnings and the triumph of light over darkness, with lamps (diyas) at every door, rangoli, sweets and family dinners.
